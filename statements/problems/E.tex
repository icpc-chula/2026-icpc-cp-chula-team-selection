\begin{problem}{E}{Entropy}{1 second}

\noindent
Professor Agasa has recently upgraded Conan's Turbo Engine Scooter with an experimental ``Particle Accelerator'' allowing it to travel through space and time. However, the engine's core is currently unstable because its energy particles are scattered across the grid. To start the scooter, Conan must recalibrate the particle accelerator system by finding a single focal point where the maximum number of particles can gather.

\noindent
The particle accelerator currently contains $N$ energy particles. Each particle $i$ is located at the coordinates $(x_i, y_i, z_i)$ and has an entropy fluctuation level of $d_i$.

\noindent
Because of the unstable entropy, a particle can only move in discrete jumps. In each step, a particle can teleport from its current position to a new coordinate $(a_i, b_i, c_i)$ when the Manhattan distance of that jump is exactly its entropy level $d_i$. A particle can perform any number of teleports (including zero) to reach its destination.

\noindent
Conan needs to select a final convergence point $(p, q, r)$ to gather the particles. So he asks you to find the maximum number of particles that can teleport to the exact same point $(p, q, r)$ to stabilize the Turbo Engine Scooter?

\InputFile

\noindent
The first line contains a single integer $N$ ($1 \le N \le 10^5$) --- the number of energy particles inside the engine.

\noindent
The next $N$ lines contain integers $x_i,y_i,z_i,d_i$ ($|x_i|,|y_i|,|z_i| \le 10^9;0\le d_i \le 10^9$) --- the starting 3D coordinates and the entropy fluctuation level of the $i$-th particle.

\OutputFile

\noindent
Print a single integer --- the maximum number of particles that can gather at the same chosen coordinate $(p, q, r)$.

\Examples

\begin{example}
\exmp{1}{5
1 1 1 1
2 2 2 2
1 -1 10 3
4 5 0 4
5 -10 21 20
}{4
}
\end{example}
