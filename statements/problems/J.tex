\begin{problem}{J}{Just One Contact}{1 second}

\noindent
The Detective Boys have just cracked their biggest case yet. While tailing a suspicious man through Beika Park, the five of them uncovered a counterfeiting ring. Mitsuhiko has written the ring's whole chain of command in his notebook: all $N$ members, from the lowest street runner up to the boss.

\noindent
The members are numbered from $1$ to $N$. Every member except the boss takes orders from exactly one handler: member $i$ reports to member $P_i$. The boss reports to no one, so Mitsuhiko writes $P_i = i$ for the boss. Two members are in contact if one of them is the other's handler. Following handlers upward from any member always leads to the boss.

\noindent
Inspector Megure's team takes the notebook and plans a dragnet, but Prof. Agasa warns them that the ring is careful. A member who still has two or more contacts at large is safe: one contact gives them an alibi while another helps them get away. A member who has at most one contact still at large, however, can be cornered.

\noindent
So every morning at dawn, Detectives Takagi and Sato lead a single coordinated raid. At the same moment, they arrest every member still at large who has at most one contact still at large. This includes any member who has no contacts left at all. Since all of a morning's arrests happen at once, two members who are each other's last contact are arrested together on the same morning. The boss gets no special protection: if the boss has at most one contact at large, the boss is arrested too.

\noindent
Each raid can leave other members exposed, and those members are picked up the next morning. Inspector Megure has promised the Detective Boys a feast of eel rice once the last member is behind bars, and Genta can hardly wait. How many mornings will it take until every member of the ring has been arrested?

\InputFile

\noindent
The first line contains a single integer $N$ ($1 \le N \le 100\,000$)~--- the number of members of the ring.

\noindent
The $i$-th of the next $N$ lines contains a single integer $P_i$ ($1 \le P_i \le N$)~--- the handler of member $i$. If $P_i = i$, then member $i$ is the boss.

\OutputFile

\noindent
Print a single integer~--- the number of mornings until every member of the ring has been arrested.
